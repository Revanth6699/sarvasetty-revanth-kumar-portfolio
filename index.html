03 / WORKING APPROACH

A model is one part
of a working system.

I like projects where the modelling has to connect to something real: a decision, a service, a dashboard or a workflow that can be tested end to end.

01

Understand the question

Define the problem, inputs, constraints and what a useful result should mean.

02

Prepare and model

Build the data flow, choose an appropriate model and evaluate it against clear tests.

03

Connect the pieces

Expose results through APIs, persistence, streaming or a small user-facing interface.

04

Validate the system

Check edge cases, keep the workflow reproducible and make outputs easy to inspect.
